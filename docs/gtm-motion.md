# Windansea Coconuts — GTM Motion Plan

**Pivot:** From one-off branded coconut activations → recurring data-driven field marketing partner for B2B tech companies.

**Position:** Experiential lead-gen agency with the attribution rigor of a performance marketing firm. Coconuts are the signature activation device, not the product.

**Constraints:**
- Team: 2 co-owners, no FTEs, seasonal contractors for events
- Geography: US coasts (SF Bay, LA, NYC, Boston) — covers ~80% of B2B tech conferences
- Capital: investor interest, but bootstrap-realistic plan first
- Proof: one closed $25K project deal, no recurring contracts yet

---

## 1. The Strategic Bet

Field marketing for B2B tech is a $4-6B/year category dominated by two extremes:
- **Generic experiential agencies** that can't prove ROI (creative-led, retainer-heavy, attribution-allergic)
- **Performance marketing firms** that don't do in-person (digital-only, no event craft)

The gap: **experiential agencies that can prove pipeline impact.** That's the lane Windansea takes.

The coconut is a Trojan horse. It gets attention, generates dwell time at booths, creates content moments, and produces a memorable scan-to-coconut lead capture mechanic. But the *product* is a measurable lead engine, not a beach prop.

**Positioning statement:**
> Windansea is the field marketing partner for B2B tech companies who want experiential warmth at conferences and the pipeline reporting of a digital channel. We run your event program end-to-end, capture leads through our signature coconut activation, and report on closed-won revenue you'd otherwise lose to "influenced" attribution.

## 2. ICP — Who We Sell To in Year 1

Selling to "all tech companies" is what every founder says before getting humbled. We narrow.

### Year-1 ICP (primary)
- **Mid-market B2B SaaS, $20-150M ARR**
- **Has a VP/Director of Field Marketing or Demand Gen** (not just a CMO who'd outsource thinking)
- **Spends $500K-$3M/year on events** (10-25 events on the calendar)
- **HubSpot or Salesforce** as CRM (both = easy attribution integration)
- **Sales cycle 3-9 months** (long enough to need attribution help, short enough to see ROI inside a year)
- **Conference circuit:** SaaStr, INBOUND, RSA, AWS re:Invent, Money 20/20, Dreamforce, Black Hat, Web Summit, dev-tool flagships (Render/Vercel/etc.)

### Disqualifiers
- Pre-Series B (no event budget, no field marketer)
- Mega-enterprise (Salesforce, Snowflake) — locked into mdg/GPJ/Opus, multi-year RFPs, not winnable in year 1
- Consumer brands (different buying motion)
- Companies whose CRM is a spreadsheet (no attribution = no upside)

### Buying triggers
- Just hired a Head of Field Marketing
- Q4 budget planning (Sep-Nov) for following year's event calendar
- Bad experience with prior event vendor / agency churn
- New CMO setting up ROI rigor
- Conference season starting (Jan/Feb panic for spring events)

### Personas
| Buyer | Why they care | Decision power |
|---|---|---|
| **VP/Head of Field Marketing** | Career-defining: prove field events drive pipeline | Owner of the budget, runs the eval |
| **CMO** | Replaces a line item with a measurable one | Approves, doesn't run process |
| **VP Sales** | Wants leads scored and routed cleanly | Influences, blocks if reporting is weak |
| **RevOps** | CRM access, attribution integration | Technical veto power — must love us |

## 4. Offer & Packaging

Three tiers. All built on **MSA + SOW** structure. No à la carte unless strategic.

### Tier 1: Activation (entry / proof)
- **Price:** $25-50K per event
- **Includes:** Booth coconut activation, on-site staff (2-3), lead capture mechanic, basic CRM upload, post-event report
- **Purpose:** Land-and-expand on-ramp. Sell once → convert to retainer in 60 days
- **Not the long-term product** — these are pilots

### Tier 2: Program (the core offer)
- **Price:** $12-20K/month retainer + pass-throughs (booth fees, travel, materials)
- **12-month commitment**
- **Includes:**
  - Event strategy & calendar planning (6-10 events/year)
  - Pre-event: booth design, attendee outreach, materials sourcing
  - On-site execution: coconuts, staff, lead capture
  - Post-event: lead qualification, CRM upload, qualified-meeting handoff to sales
  - **Quarterly pipeline report** tied to client CRM closed-won data
- **Sweet spot for year 1 — this is what we're really selling**

### Tier 3: Program + Performance (year 2+, with anchor clients only)
- **Price:** Reduced retainer ($8-12K/mo) + 5-8% of first-year ACV on attributed closed-won deals
- **Requires:** CRM read access, attribution contract terms, 6+ months of trust
- **Purpose:** Aligns incentives, upside for both sides, defensible moat

### Contract structure
- **MSA** (Master Services Agreement) signed once: IP, data handling, attribution rules, payment terms, termination
- **SOW** per quarter or event: scope, deliverables, fees
- **Annual renewal cycle**, negotiated in Q4

### Pass-through policy
Always billed at cost + 10% admin. Booth fees, travel, hotels, materials, shipping. Clients expect this. Don't bury it in retainer — it kills your margin and erodes trust when they audit.

## 7. Conversion Play: The $25K Client → Annual Program

The single highest-leverage move in the next 60 days. This client is the most likely yes you'll ever have.

### The pitch (45 min meeting, 30 days post-event)
1. **Reflect (5 min):** What worked, what didn't, qualitative
2. **Show the data (15 min):** Leads captured → MQL → SAL → opps. If they'll share closed-won, even better
3. **Reframe (10 min):** "You spent $25K and got X. For $12-15K/month, you'd get 6-8 events at this caliber + integrated reporting + a calendar partner."
4. **Propose (10 min):** 2026 program SOW — specific events, specific scope, monthly retainer, 12-month commitment
5. **Close (5 min):** "What needs to be true for you to sign by [date]?"

### Pricing the offer
- Anchor at $15K/mo retainer + pass-throughs for 8 events
- Walk-away floor: $10K/mo for 6 events
- Sweetener: "Lock in 2026 pricing now, before our rates increase in Q3"

### Backup plays if they balk at retainer
- 3-event package for $60K (still progress vs. one-offs)
- 6-month pilot retainer at $10K/mo before full year
- Per-event with right-of-first-refusal on their 2026 calendar

## 8. Attribution Approach (MVP, no custom build year 1)

Don't build software. Use the client's CRM. Period.

### The minimum viable attribution stack
1. **Pre-event:** Set up a "Windansea — [Event Name]" lead source/campaign in client's HubSpot or Salesforce
2. **On-site capture:** Badge scanner or QR-to-form, every lead tagged at source with that campaign ID
3. **Same-day upload:** Leads in client CRM within 24h, properly tagged
4. **Quarterly closed-loop:** Client (or RevOps) pulls a report — opps/closed-won where source = Windansea
5. **Quarterly report:** We assemble the narrative; the data lives in their system

### Tools (off-the-shelf, no build)
- **Badge scanners:** iCapture, Captello, Cvent LeadCapture (~$300-1500/event)
- **CRM integration:** Native HubSpot/Salesforce APIs — Zapier or built-in
- **Reporting:** Looker Studio / HubSpot dashboard / Salesforce report — shared view, not custom software
- **Pipeline tracking:** if client lets us in, we use their CRM directly; if not, monthly self-reported summary with audit clause

### When to build custom (year 2+)
- 5+ clients on the program
- Manual reconciliation taking >10 hrs/week
- Clients asking for a portal
- Revenue-share deals requiring trusted third-party attribution

Until then: nail the manual workflow. Document it. Hire a Solutions Engineer / RevOps person before building software.
